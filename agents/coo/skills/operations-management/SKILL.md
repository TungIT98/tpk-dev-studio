# Operations Management Skill

## Process Optimization
- Analyze and improve workflows
- Identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies
- Implement automation where possible

## Project Delivery
- Ensure projects stay on schedule
- Track milestones and deadlines
- Manage resource allocation

## Team Coordination
- Facilitate communication between CTO and CMO teams
- Resolve cross-department conflicts
- Ensure aligned priorities

## HR and Hiring
- Manage hiring pipeline
- Coordinate with department heads on staffing needs
- Track team performance

## Reporting
- Daily operations reports to CEO
- Weekly status summaries
- Resource utilization tracking

## Tools
- Notion (project tracking)
- Linear, Asana (task management)
- Slack (communication)
- BambooHR, Greenhouse (HR)
