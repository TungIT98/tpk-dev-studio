## Thumbnail Specs (for Discovery Ads)

### TikTok Discovery
- Format: 9:16 (1080x1920)
- Text: White bold text, black outline, 20% padding
- Gameplay: Fill frame, no text overlap bottom 25%
- Logo: Top right, 5% of frame

### YouTube Thumbnail
- Format: 16:9 (1280x720) or 2:1 (1200x630)
- Text: Sans-serif, high contrast (yellow/white on dark)
- Expression: Use player face/reaction when possible
- Branding: TKP logo bottom right, 10% width max

### Instagram Feed
- Format: 1:1 (1080x1080) + 4:5 (1080x1350) versions
- Style: Clean, minimal text, vibrant colors
- CTA: "Play link in bio" or swipe up

## A/B Creative Testing Cadence

### Week 1-2: Baseline
- Launch 3 hook variants per game per platform
- Set even budget split
- Let algorithms learn

### Week 3: First Optimization
- Kill bottom 33% performers (lowest CTR/video retention)
- Double budget on top performer
- Keep middle 33% running

### Week 4: Refresh
- Pause all original creatives
- Launch new creative variants based on learnings
- Document winning elements for next cycle

## Conversion Funnel — Ad to Play

```
AD IMPRESSION
    ↓
VIDEO PLAY (auto-muted on TikTok, sound-on on YouTube)
    ↓
HOOK (0-3s) — Stop the scroll
    ↓
GAMEPLAY SHOWCASE (3-15s)
    ↓
CTA CLICK → LANDING PAGE
    ↓
GAME LAUNCH SCREEN
    ↓
PLAY SESSION STARTED (Conversion!)
    ↓
[RETARGET] Inactive users → 7-day retargeting campaign
```

### Landing Page Requirements
- Load time: < 3 seconds (mobile)
- CTA above fold: "Play Now" / "Start Free"
- Auto-launch game (one tap max)
- Social proof: "500K+ players" badge
- Clear trust signals (no ads, safe for kids)
