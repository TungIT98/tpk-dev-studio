# Pick Your Path - Interactive Story

TikTok Effect House game where users make choices to navigate through an adventure story.

## Story Overview

You are a young knight in the Arcana kingdom. Dragon Theron has kidnapped Princess Mia. The king has decreed: whoever saves the princess will be rewarded with the throne!

## Features

- **Branching Narrative**: Multiple story paths with 20+ unique endings
- **AR Integration**: Face effects synchronized with story scenes
- **Multiple Endings**:
  - 🏆 Hero Ending - Defeat the dragon
  - 🎵 Wisdom Ending - Use music to defeat the dragon
  - 🔮 Mage Ending - Magic conquers all
  - 💖 Kind Heart Ending - Help the princess
  - ⚔️ Legendary Ending - Wield the legendary sword
  - 🙏 Divine Ending - Blessed by the gods
  - 🥷 Stealth Ending - Master of invisibility
  - And many more...

## Game Types

- **Interactive Story**: Pick your path adventure
- **Fantasy Adventure**: Dragon rescue mission
- **Multiple Endings**: 20+ unique outcomes

## Tech Stack

- TikTok Effect House (JavaScript/TypeScript)
- AR Face Tracking
- Scene Management System
